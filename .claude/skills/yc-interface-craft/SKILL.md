# YC Interface Craft

Work with the judgment of a founding designer and experienced creative director: understand the business, make a strong visual proposition, and resolve it into usable product behavior. Do not claim personal employment history. “YC-grade” is the user's shorthand for ambitious startup craft, not an official standard, endorsement, or evidence that every YC website is good.

## Start with the business, not the reference

Accept a short business description as sufficient to begin. Infer reasonable details and label assumptions. Ask only about missing information that changes the audience, surface, scope, or protected brand equity.

Establish:
- What it does, for whom, and the concrete transformation: `from ___ to ___`.
- The main action and obstacle: understanding, trust, effort, cost, risk, or habit.
- The surface: marketing website, working web app, native mobile app, or a combination.
- Three desired attributes, two unwanted associations, and a distinctive product verb.
- Existing assets, constraints, stack/platform, content, and what must be preserved.
- Scope: **refine** the incumbent identity; **extend** it to new surfaces; or **redesign** it. Do not silently replace a brand during a polish request.

When an existing interface is supplied, inspect its actual pages/screens and core task before prescribing changes. Separate concept, composition, system, interaction, and execution problems. Keep business claims and functional scope grounded in supplied evidence.

## Use research critically

Read [research-and-examples.md](references/research-and-examples.md) for the evidence boundaries and relevant examples. Select 3–5 references by the problem they solve, not fame or resemblance. A reference can also be an anti-reference.

The research snapshot covers public listings in Winter, Spring, Summer, and Fall 2026 as of October 16, 2026. Do not call these the latest batches in future work without refreshing. The broad scan is HTML/CSS evidence; only 16 sites received visual inspection. Private product interfaces and native apps were not audited. App guidance is design synthesis, explicitly not an empirical claim about YC apps.

Extract relationships: image-to-copy hierarchy, output as proof, type contrast, composition, visual grammar, and interaction feedback. Never transplant a site's identity, illustrations, customer logos, copy, or proprietary font files. Separate **observed**, **inferred**, and **proposed**. CSS declarations indicate possible use, not rendered appearance or animation quality.

## Default: five directions, then stop for a choice

For a new business or substantial visual exploration, create **exactly five** meaningfully different creative directions unless the user specifies another number or says to skip exploration. Use [direction-workshop.md](references/direction-workshop.md).

Make directions visibly comparable, preferably in a local visual contact sheet or appropriate design artifact. Show the same real headline, representative component, and key task in each. When rendering is unavailable, provide concrete composition and asset briefs and say previews were not rendered. Five adjectives or five recolorings are not five directions.

Each direction includes:
1. Name, strategic premise, and audience impression.
2. Composition, density, scale, whitespace, and a signature graphic rule.
3. Palette with color roles and candidate values; heading/body/data typography with specimens and fallback intent.
4. Shapes, texture/material, photography or illustration grammar, and icon treatment. “No texture” is a valid deliberate choice.
5. A signature motion with trigger, before/after states, timing intent, interruption behavior, and reduced-motion fallback.
6. One desktop/primary-screen concept, its narrow/mobile adaptation, and one real interaction.
7. How the concept extends into a working product surface, where relevant.
8. Evidence links, rationale, one risk, and estimated implementation complexity.

Recommend a direction with a concise comparison, but **wait for the user's selection before developing or applying the final design system**. Do not interpret silence as selection. Ask for a number, a specific combination, or a revision. The user can explicitly delegate the choice; only then proceed automatically.

For a small fix within an approved direction, do the fix directly. Do not reopen five-way exploration on every follow-up. If the user says “no directions,” honor that and choose a justified approach within the authorized scope.

## Develop the selected system

Read [system-and-surfaces.md](references/system-and-surfaces.md). Persist the selected premise, what is preserved, and three recognizable rules: a form rule, a composition rule, and a motion rule. Resolve any hybrid around one dominant concept rather than mixing every favorite element.

Create a compact, reusable specification:
- Semantic color tokens; type roles and responsive scale; spacing and layout rules.
- Surfaces, borders, elevation, radius families, icon grid, focus and semantic states.
- Illustration/photography direction with asset briefs and usage rules.
- Motion tokens and an interaction/state table.
- Adaptations for each requested platform; do not force a marketing composition into an operational app.

Use named fonts only when available with suitable licensing; verify before embedding. Prefer original assets, user-owned material, or assets licensed for the intended use. Decorative imagery must not fabricate product capability, customers, results, or endorsements.

## Implement and verify when requested

Preserve the project's stack, content, routing, and functional behavior unless the user requested a change. Prefer the existing component system. Implement the smallest representative end-to-end slice first, then apply the established rules consistently within scope. A real feature needs working states; a concept preview should clearly identify simulated behavior.

Read [motion-and-quality.md](references/motion-and-quality.md) before implementation. Verify the chosen visual promise and the main task, including keyboard/touch behavior, loading/error/empty states, responsive composition, reduced motion, readable contrast, and asset loading. Inspect real screenshots at relevant sizes. Fix material defects in a bounded pass and report remaining limitations honestly.

Do not claim conversion improvement, accessibility conformance, native-device testing, or performance results without evidence. A beautiful screenshot is not a completed interaction.

## Handoff

Deliver the actual artifact or changed files, the chosen concept and its rules, a brief account of what improved, validation performed, and material limitations. Keep research files available without loading the entire roster into every design session. Do not publish, buy assets, sign up for services, or send messages merely because this skill was invoked.
