## Two ways to build

1. **AI organization in Grok Bot.** The Product Manager keeps one shared brief. The other roles are added as needed (see the org chart in HACKATHON_PLAN.md §2.1). Each breakout deck has a setup prompt.
2. **"Build with a coding agent."** 01 Your brief → 02 Your website → 03 Your marketing. "The workshop guide uses Claude Code." Deck: https://www.shipai.club/workshops/vibe-coding-gtm/materials/deck

The worked example is *Porchlight Plant Care*, a fictional business: Product Manager → Product Designer → Frontend Engineer.

## Judging: "Design. Craft. Receipts."

Three criteria. Each is scored 1–5, with equal weight.

| Criterion | What judges look for |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **Design** | Clear positioning, useful content, and a site that is easy to navigate. |
| **Craft** | Polished details, and a site that works well on desktop and mobile. |
| **Receipts** | Evidence of the pages, working features and improvements shipped during the weekend. Show live pages, working features and before-and-after evidence. Growth metrics are not required. |

## Prizes

1st place: $200 plus up to 4 Workuity seats ($1,200 value each). 2nd place: $150. 3rd place: $50. Every team that submits gets a project listing and a certification. You keep your IP.
