## 3. Patterns we see (ESTIMATE, from the table)

1. **"Free" leads every offer.** TurboTenant ("Get Started Free"), Avail ($0 per unit) and Baselane (free accounts) put the price in the hook or the button. RentRedi has no free plan and uses a money-back guarantee instead.
2. **"Like a pro" is taken.** TurboTenant's campaign tagline and Avail's headline both use "like a pro". Proppros should avoid that phrase.
3. **Payments and listings are the center.** Competitors lead with rent collection, screening and listing syndication. None of the landing pages we saw leads with maintenance, vendors or records. This supports Proppros Option B ("run the property, not just the payments").
4. **Big-number social proof.** All four show user counts or ratings ("1M+ landlords", "4.8/5"). **Proppros has no such proof** (brief §4). Our ads must use the product itself and the founder story instead.
5. **Vacancy help is framed as syndication count** ("25+ sites", "19 sites"). Proppros does **not** syndicate to listing sites. Its vacancy pillar is a different promise: a listing page, ready-to-post ad text, a share image, a QR flyer and online applications. Never imply syndication.
6. **Celebrity and TV.** TurboTenant uses a national celebrity spot. We cannot compete on that. A plain, screen-led, founder-voiced ad is the realistic contrast.

## 5. Housing and fair-housing rules

**What Meta requires (VERIFIED):**
- Advertisers in or targeting the US, Canada or parts of Europe must self-declare a Special Ad Category for housing, employment and financial ads, and use only approved targeting. Source: Meta Advertising Standards, https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/ (no page date).
- Under that category, age is fixed at 18–65+, gender cannot be chosen, locations need at least a 15-mile radius, ZIP codes and location exclusions are not supported, detailed targeting exclusions are not allowed, and lookalike audiences are unavailable. Source: Meta Marketing API, Special Ad Category, https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/special-ad-category/ (US requirement in effect since 2019-12-04).
- Meta's own pages that we could read say "ads about housing" and "offer housing ... opportunities", and give home listing catalogs as an example. **They do not say whether software sold to landlords is a housing ad.** Third-party summaries (not Meta) describe the category as covering sale or rental of homes, mortgage and homeowners insurance, housing repair, and appraisal services: https://ppc.land/special-ad-category/.
- Since a 2022 US Department of Justice settlement, Meta runs a "Variance Reduction System" on all housing ads and removed "Special Ad Audiences". Source: NAR, https://www.nar.realtor/washington-report/meta-implements-new-housing-ads-platform-after-doj-fair-housing-settlement ; HousingWire, https://www.housingwire.com/articles/meta-rolls-out-new-system-to-address-housing-ad-discrimination/ .

**Our reading (HYPOTHESIS, not legal advice):** a Proppros ad sells software to landlords. It does not offer a home to rent. It is probably **not** a housing ad. But the vacancy-pillar creative shows rentals, "For rent" flyers and listing pages, and Meta's review may flag it as housing.

**Recommendation:** build the campaign so it works either way.
- Target in a way that already fits the housing rules: US-wide, ages 18–65+, all genders, no ZIP codes, no exclusions, no lookalikes.
- Do not declare the Housing category at first. If Meta rejects or flags an ad as housing, declare it and re-submit. The targeting does not change, so the test survives.
- Show only fictional listings from the showcase database. No real addresses.
- Any ad text shown inside the creative (for example, a sample listing ad) describes the home, never the people. No "perfect for families", "ideal for students", "no kids", "Christians", "walking distance to church" or similar. This matches the vacancy kit's fair-housing-safe wording (brief §2b).
- Owner check: confirm with Meta support or counsel if the owner wants certainty. Open question Q-A in the handoff.

## 6. Recommended launch campaign (one campaign, one channel)

### Channel: Meta (Facebook + Instagram), Feed and Reels placements — HYPOTHESIS

Why Meta and not Google Search:
1. **The two assets in scope (brief D9) are a 4:5 static and a 9:16 video.** These are native to Meta Feed and Reels. Search ads use text only, so they would not test the creative we are making.
2. **The audience is not searching for Proppros yet.** Search terms like "free property management software" are already owned by competitor content pages (TurboTenant page dated 2026-09-09). A new brand with no reviews and a 3-unit free cap would bid against "unlimited units, free" offers on the same results page (RESEARCH H4). ESTIMATE.
3. **Meta lets the product screen do the selling.** Patterns 3 and 5 show a gap (maintenance, records, vacancy kit). A screen-led video can show that gap. A 30-character search headline cannot.
4. Known risk: Meta may treat vacancy creative as a housing ad (section 5). The targeting plan already fits those rules.

### Campaign set-up — HYPOTHESIS

| Item | Plan |
|------|------|
| Objective | Sales (or Leads) objective, **optimized for one conversion event: account created**. Not clicks, not landing-page views. |
| Conversion event | "Account created" fires when a new user reaches `/welcome` after the sign-up form (the form on `/register` sends new accounts to `/welcome`). Google sign-in should also land on `/welcome`; the Frontend Engineer must confirm. |
| Tracking needed | A Meta Pixel or Conversions API event for "account created". **Brief D7 says "no analytics this round".** Optimizing for sign-ups is not possible without this event. **Owner decision needed.** Without it, the only option is a manual count of new accounts per day, which cannot tell which ad drove them. |
| Structure | 1 campaign, 1 ad set, 2 ads (Static A, Video B). Advantage+ placements limited to Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels. |
| Audience | United States. Ages 18–65+. All genders. Broad targeting (no interests), or at most interests from Meta's approved list such as "rental property" or "landlord". No exclusions, no lookalikes. See audience notes below. |
| Landing pages | Static A (vacancy angle) → `https://proppros.vercel.app/features/vacancies`. Video B (one calm month) → `https://proppros.vercel.app/` (home, where the month timeline lives). Each link carries UTM tags (`utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=launch_v1&utm_content=static_a` or `video_b`). |
| Ad text (primary, under 125 characters) | A: "Create one listing. Get a listing page, ready-to-post ad text, a share image and a flyer with a QR code." (104) · B: "Rent, leases, repairs and vacancies in one place. Built by a landlord who runs three rentals with it." (101) |
| Headline (under 27 characters for Facebook Feed) | A: "Fill your vacancy, free" (23) · B: "One calm month" (14) |
| Button | "Sign up" (Meta button that matches "Start free") |
| Budget | **HYPOTHESIS, needs the owner's explicit approval before any spend:** $20 per day for 14 days = **$280 total**, split by Meta across the two ads. This is a learning budget, not a benchmark. We have no cost-per-sign-up data. |

### The test (falsifiable)

- **Metric:** cost per completed sign-up (spend ÷ "account created" events), plus post-click sign-up rate (accounts created ÷ landing-page views) so we can tell an ad problem from a page problem.
- **Comparison:** Video B ("one calm month", home page) vs. Static A ("fill your vacancy", vacancy page), same ad set, same audience, same dates.
- **Expected direction:** Video B has a **lower cost per completed sign-up** than Static A. Reason: it shows the whole product (rent, lease, repairs, vacancy) in one story, so it attracts landlords who want an all-in-one tool, which matches the free plan. Static A may get cheaper clicks from landlords with a vacancy who only want a listing.
- **It loses if:** Static A has an equal or lower cost per completed sign-up at the end of the test.
- **Decision rule (ESTIMATE, set before launch):** do not judge an ad before it has 1,000+ impressions (ad-creative skill guidance). Call the test only if the two ads together reach at least 20 account-created events; otherwise report "inconclusive" and do not claim a winner.
- **Known confound:** A and B differ in format, angle and landing page. A win tells us which **package** to keep, not which single variable caused it. Next round: hold the format and change only the angle.

### Audience notes

- Wording in all copy: "independent landlords" or "small landlords". **No unit counts** (brief §2b). The 3-unit limit appears only on the pricing page and FAQ, not in ads.
- Do not target or exclude by age, gender, family status, ZIP code or any protected trait, even though software ads may allow it. This keeps us safe if Meta reclassifies the ads as housing.
- Expect some renters to see the ads (broad targeting). The copy speaks to people who own and rent out homes ("your tenant", "your rental") so the ad filters by itself.
- Landing page match (cro skill): the vacancy page must show "Start free" above the fold on a phone, and the home hero must keep "Start free" visible. The sign-up form has 4 fields (name, email, password of 10+ characters, optional organization) plus Google sign-in. Marking "organization" clearly as optional is a quick win to test later, not now (one variable at a time).

## 8. Creative brief A — static, 4:5 — for Content Creator - Design

**Angle:** Fill your vacancy (new pillar, brief §2b). Grounding: site section `VACANCY` and feature `vacancies` in `content.ts`; competitor gap in pattern 5.

| Slot | Copy |
|------|------|
| Hook (top line, small, copper) | "Got a unit coming open?" |
| Headline (max 8 words) | **"One listing. Everything you need to fill it."** (7 words) |
| Supporting line | "A listing page, ready-to-post ad text, a share image, a QR flyer and online applications." |
| CTA (button shape in the image) | "Start free" |
| Small print | "No card needed." |

**Format and size:** 1440 x 1800 px (4:5), PNG. Also export 1080 x 1350.

**Visual direction (Strata brand):**
- Background: dark `#111217`, full bleed. One copper accent `#EDAF88` for the hook, the CTA button fill and one thin rule. Body text in off-white.
- Type: headline in **Archivo expanded** (heavy weight, 2 lines max, left-aligned, top third). Hook, supporting line and CTA in **DM Sans**.
- Layout, top to bottom: hook → headline → supporting line (top 40%); product composition (middle 45%); CTA button + "No card needed." + Proppros wordmark (bottom 15%, kept above Meta's CTA bar).
- Product composition: a laptop-free, flat "kit" layout. Left: the **vacancies screen** (published listing with its views and applications). Right, overlapping slightly: the **share image** and the **printable flyer with QR code** at a small angle, as if laid on a desk. Soft copper glow behind, no stock photos, no people.
- **Screens:** `frontend/public/screens/` is **empty** today. Use a capture from the fictional showcase database only (brief §2b), never the local database. The screen to capture: Proppros **Vacancies** page with one published listing (fictional address, for example "12 Linden Court, Unit B"), its status "Published", a view count and an applications count, matching the site alt text "The vacancies page in Proppros with a published listing, its views and applications". Flyer and share image: generate them from that same fictional listing in the product.
- Any visible listing ad text must describe the home only (beds, baths, rent, features). No words about who should live there.
- Contrast: all text must pass WCAG AA on `#111217`. Keep text off the outer 6% on each side.

**Alt text:** "Proppros ad. Headline: One listing. Everything you need to fill it. The Proppros vacancies screen with a published listing, next to a share image and a printable flyer with a QR code. Button: Start free."

**Do not:** show phone app store badges, rent payment buttons, "syndicate" or "post to 20 sites", user counts, real addresses or faces.

## 9. Creative brief B — video, 9:16, 15–25 seconds — for Content Creator - Video

**Common specs:** 1080 x 1920, MP4 H.264, 30 fps. Burned-in captions (most feeds play muted). All on-screen copy inside the safe area: 14% top, 35% bottom, 6% sides. Copy in DM Sans, titles in Archivo expanded, background `#111217`, copper `#EDAF88` accents. Screens from the showcase database only. Music: licensed or none. If a voice is used, it is a plain, calm voice reading the on-screen copy; do not imply it is the founder unless the founder records it.

### Concept 1 — "One calm month" (RECOMMENDED) — 22 s

Grounding: `MONTH` timeline in `content.ts` (the approved "How it works" section).

**Hook (0–2 s):** A calendar day-counter snaps to "1st" with the on-screen line **"Rent day. Nothing to type."** and the ledger shows rent charges already posted.

| Time | Scene | On-screen copy |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 0–2 s | Calendar flips to **1st**. Rent ledger: monthly charges appear on each lease. | "1st. Rent charges post on their own." |
| 2–5 s | Flip to **3rd**. Lease with "Signed" status and the signed PDF. | "3rd. Lease signed online." |
| 5–8 s | Flip to **6th**. Dashboard with a balance due highlighted (garnet tag). | "6th. See who is behind." |
| 8–12 s | Flip to **12th**. Maintenance request with a photo of a leak; a vendor is assigned to the work order. | "12th. A leak, with photos. Vendor assigned." |
| 12–16 s | Flip to **20th**. Vacancy listing page, then the QR flyer slides in. | "20th. Vacancy shared. Applications come in." |
| 16–19 s | Flip to **31st**. Profit and loss report, "Export CSV". | "31st. Books ready for your accountant." |
| 19–22 s | End card: Proppros wordmark on `#111217`, copper button. | "One calm month. Start free." · small: "No card needed. Built by a landlord." |

**CTA:** "Start free" (end card) + Meta "Sign up" button.
**Why recommended:** it shows the full all-in-one promise in one story, uses an approved site section (message match with the home page), and every step is a live feature.

### Concept 2 — "Fill your vacancy" — 18 s

Grounding: `VACANCY` section and `vacancies` feature in `content.ts`.

**Hook (0–2 s):** A blank "FOR RENT" sign on a dark screen; text types in: **"Unit open next month?"**

| Time | Scene | On-screen copy |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 0–2 s | Blank "For rent" sign graphic, copper outline. | "Unit open next month?" |
| 2–5 s | A listing is created: photos drop into the listing form. | "Create one listing." |
| 5–8 s | The public listing page, with its share link copied. | "Get a page you can share." |
| 8–11 s | Ready-to-post ad text appears; a "Copy" button is tapped. | "Ad text, ready to post." |
| 11–14 s | Share image and flyer with QR code side by side; a phone scans the QR and opens the listing. | "Share image. Flyer with a QR code." |
| 14–16 s | Applications list with documents attached. | "Applications in one list." |
| 16–18 s | End card. | "Fill your vacancy. Start free." · small: "No card needed." |

**CTA:** "Start free" (end card) + Meta "Sign up" button. Landing page: `/features/vacancies`.
**Note:** the ad text on screen must describe the home only. No syndication claim.

### Recommendation

Make **Concept 1** first. It is the Video B used in the test (section 6). Concept 2 is the back-up, and the natural follow-up test (same format, different angle) after launch.
