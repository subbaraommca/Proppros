Features

Everything a small landlord does, in one place.

Leases with e-signature

Use templates, sign online and keep every term, date and document together.

Rent ledger

Charges post each month. Record payments, send receipts and see who is behind.

Maintenance

Tenants report issues with photos. Track work orders, vendors, quotes and invoices.

Vacancies

Share a listing page and take rental applications online.

Tenant portal

Tenants see their lease, balance and documents, and send requests and messages.

Reports

Profit and loss, rent roll and expenses by property. Export to CSV at tax time.
