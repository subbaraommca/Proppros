Keep tenant communication in one place, with a record of everything you sent.

## Messages

**Messages → New message** starts a conversation with a tenant. They get it in the tenant portal and by email, and by text if they've turned texts on, and can reply from the portal. Tenants can also start a conversation from **Messages** in their portal. Unread conversations show a count next to **Messages** in the sidebar.

Repairs belong in **Maintenance**, so they're tracked with photos and status. Use messages for everything else: questions, parking, move-in details.

## Announcements

**Messages → Announcements** sends one message to every tenant with an active lease at a property, or at all your properties. Use it for water shut-offs, pest treatments or parking-lot work. Announcements go out by email (and text, if you choose) and appear on tenants' portal home page for 90 days.

## Formal notices

On a lease, open **Notices → Send a notice** and choose a template:

- **Rent increase:** new rent and effective date.
- **Notice of entry:** date, time window and reason.
- **Lease violation:** what's wrong and how many days to fix it.
- **Pay rent or quit:** the amount past due (defaults to the lease balance) and days to pay.
- **General notice:** your own wording.

Preview the filled-in notice, edit the wording if needed, and send. The notice is saved as a PDF on the lease, shared in the tenant portal, emailed with the PDF attached and optionally texted. You can see when the tenant opened it in the portal.

Notice rules (how much warning, exact wording, and whether it must be handed over or mailed) vary by state and city. Check your local requirements, especially for pay-or-quit notices.

## Text messages (SMS)

Tenants turn texts on themselves in the portal (**Messages → Text messages**), agreeing to receive them. Replying **STOP** turns texts off, and tenants' text replies arrive in their conversation. Texts are sent through Twilio once you add Twilio to Proppros (see the setup guide). Until then, texts are only logged.

## Move-out notices and renter's insurance

Tenants can **give move-out notice** from their lease page in the portal. You'll see a banner on the lease; **Acknowledge** confirms it to the tenant and can set the lease end date.

Tenants upload **renter's insurance** with its expiry date. The lease page shows each tenant's insurance status, and tenants get a reminder 14 days before it expires.
