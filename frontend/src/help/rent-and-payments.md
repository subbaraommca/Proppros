Each lease has a running **ledger**: charges add to the balance, payments and credits reduce it.

## Recording a payment

Open the lease and click **Record payment**. Choose the method (Zelle, check, cash…), the date it arrived and an optional reference such as a check number. If the tenant has an email address, they get a PDF receipt automatically.

## Charges, credits and corrections

- **Charge / credit** adds a one-off entry — for example prorated rent for a partial first month, or a repair reimbursement.
- Removing an automatic rent or late-fee charge adds a *reversal* rather than deleting it, so the automation never re-posts it.
- **Void** a manual payment entered by mistake; it disappears from the ledger. Online payments are refunded in Stripe instead.

## Late fees

Set a flat amount or a percentage of rent, plus a grace period, on the lease. One late fee is added per month, only if a balance remains after the grace period.

## Online payments

When online payments are on (**Settings → Payments**), tenants can pay by bank transfer or card from their portal, and set up autopay. Bank payments show as *Processing* for 4–5 business days and are credited once they clear.
