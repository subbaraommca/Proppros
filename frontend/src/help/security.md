## Two-factor authentication

Add a second step to sign-in with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, Authy…).

1. Go to **Settings → My account → Two-factor authentication** and click **Set up**.
2. Scan the QR code with your app, then enter the 6-digit code it shows.
3. **Save your recovery codes** somewhere safe. Each one works once if you lose your phone.

From then on, after your password you'll be asked for the current code from your app.

## Sign in with Google

If your administrator has enabled it, **Continue with Google** appears on the sign-in page. It links to an existing Proppros account with the same email address. Two-factor authentication still applies.

## Good habits

- Use a unique password of at least 10 characters.
- Sign out on shared computers.
- Review **Settings → Audit log** to see who changed what.
