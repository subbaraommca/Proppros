## Plans

| Plan | Units | Team members |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | up to 3 | you + 1 |
| **Pro** | unlimited, priced per unit | up to 10 |
| **Business** | unlimited, priced per unit | unlimited |

Paid plans are billed monthly for the number of active units; the count updates automatically when you add or archive units.

## Upgrading or cancelling

Open **Settings → Billing**. Choose a plan to go to secure checkout, or **Manage billing** to update your card, see invoices, change plans or cancel. If a subscription lapses, your data stays — you just can't add units beyond the free limit until it's renewed.
