The tenant portal lets your tenants help themselves.

## What tenants can do

- See their **current balance**, next due date and full **statement**
- Download **receipts** for every payment
- **Submit maintenance requests** with photos or video, and follow progress
- Read documents you've chosen to **share** (lease PDF, house rules…)
- **Pay online** and set up **autopay**, if you've turned on online payments

## Sharing documents

Documents you upload stay private unless you tick **Share with tenant** (or use the eye icon in a document list). Your private lease notes are never shown to tenants.
